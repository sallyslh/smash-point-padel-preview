Origins • Est. 2015

How bonza began

Amelia started with a single shelf and a small table of paper strips. Visitors came for a bottle and stayed for the conversation. Today we're still a small team, still in the same quiet room.

Every morning before the iron latch turns, natural daylight pours across the limestone slab. We take time to brew tea, steep raw accords, and evaluate how the seasons gently alter the skin’s chemistry. Here, scent is not industrial output; it is intimate architecture.

“We believe perfume is not a costume you put on, but an atmosphere that stays when you leave.”
— Amelia Hartley, Founder
Ethos & Principles

What we believe

Three promises behind every bottle.

01

Honest scents

We only stock what we'd wear ourselves.

No synthetic filler formulas or hollow celebrity endorsement contracts. Only authentic creations that speak for their craft.

02

Slow advice

No pressure, no rush, no hard sell.

Take testing strips on a walk around the neighborhood. Return hours later when the skin temperature has revealed the true heart.

03

Kind choices

Recyclable packaging and refillable options where possible.

From FSC-certified paper blotters to our artisan fountain refilling program that breathes perennial life into beautiful flacons.

Curatorial Protocol

Why we choose what we choose

Every candidate gets three tests: how it opens, how it lives on skin by lunch, and how it feels at dusk.

Morning

The first impression

Top notes, citrus & delicate aldehydes that introduce the composition without overwhelming the room.

Afternoon

The heart of the scent

Heart notes, orris, cedar & warm tea settling into rhythm with everyday body heat.

Evening

What stays behind

Base notes, amber, resin & quiet linen providing an intimate, lasting memory on the skin.
