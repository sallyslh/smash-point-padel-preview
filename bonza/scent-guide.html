Chronological Evaporation

Top, heart, base

A perfume unfolds in layers. Top notes appear first, heart notes after a few minutes, and base notes linger for hours.

08:00 AM · Top Lift 01:00 PM · Heart Bloom 09:00 PM · Base Sillage Resonance
Volatile Opening

Top · 0–15 minutes

Citrus, herbs, light fruit. The greeting chord that lifts immediately from the atomizer, sharp and ethereal.

Diffusion: Instantaneous · Lifespan: ~15 mins

The True Core

Heart · 15 minutes–3 hours

Florals, spice, green notes. The soulful body of the fragrance that develops once the alcohol fully evaporates into the skin.

Diffusion: Radiant · Lifespan: 3 Hours

Sillage Anchor

Base · 3 hours+

Woods, musk, amber, vanilla. The heavy, warm molecules that meld intimately with your natural warmth for the rest of the day and night.

Sensory Protocol

Five tips for choosing

Test on skin, not just paper. Wait twenty minutes. Try only three at a time. Smell coffee beans or your sleeve to reset. Sleep on it.

The Olfactive Palette Cleanser

Olfactory Fatigue is Real

After three strong notes, human receptors saturate. Take three calm inhalations over raw coffee beans or unperfumed wool to recalibrate clarity.

1

Test on skin

Chemistry changes everything. Lipids and natural warmth reshape top accords.

adjust
2

Wait

Let it settle for twenty minutes. The initial blast never reflects the day's true aura.

hourglass_empty
3

Three at a time

More confuses your nose. Test left wrist, right wrist, and one sternum blotter.

filter_3
4

Reset

Smell your sleeve or coffee beans. Neutral wool neutralizes saturated receptors.

refresh
5

Sleep on it

Love it tomorrow too. If the faint trace on your pillow evokes joy, it is yours.
