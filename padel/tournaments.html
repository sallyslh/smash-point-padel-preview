04 / ONBOARDING

HOW TO JOIN.

Sign up, get matched, play your games and climb the table. No complex administration or archaic club rules.

PHASE 01 60 SECONDS

1. REGISTER

Pick your division online in 60 seconds. Enter with your designated partner, or sign up solo as a "Free Agent" and our matchmaking algorithm pairs you according to your verified rating.

PHASE 02 AUTOMATED

2. GET MATCHED

We message you your confirmed fixtures and court allocations via WhatsApp and your club calendar. Need to shift match time? Proposed reschedules can be agreed directly inside the player portal.

PHASE 03 GAME TIME

3. PLAY

Show up, grab fresh match balls from the pro shop, compete under dusk floodlights, and log results. Scores are verified digitally on our courtside touch tablets right after match point.
